Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, through rclone running on the owner's own computer.

It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, solely to synchronize the owner's files. No data is collected, stored, shared, sold or transmitted to any third party. Access tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
